Why It Matters Where You Have Been

There are two ways to build a governance framework. The first is to read the standard, map the controls, and produce documentation. Most ISO consultants do this well.

The second is to have managed Meta's platform policy compliance programs at scale, converting ambiguous regulatory requirements into structured delivery across three sites in three countries. To have run a four-program VW Group portfolio where a governance failure meant a board-level conversation, not a compliance checkbox.

That experience does not make the standard easier to implement. It makes the framework you build actually survive contact with your organization.
